Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist

DO'S

1. Use strong and secure passwords
2. Protect your personal information (phone number, address, etc.)
3. Keep your profiles professional and accurate
4. Think before posting anything online
5. Share accurate and verified information
6. Be respectful and polite in online interactions
7. Use privacy settings to control who can see your content
8. Use social media for learning and networking


DON’Ts

1. Do not share personal or sensitive information
2. Do not click on unknown or suspicious links
3. Do not engage in online arguments or cyberbullying
4. Do not spread fake news or unverified information
5. Do not use abusive, inappropriate or offensive language
6. Do not overshare your daily activities
7. Do not use social media during classes or study time excessively
8. Do not accept requests from unknown people
