Stay Safe Online Checklist:

- Avoid sharing personal information on social media
- Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
- Don't click on unknown or suspicious links
- Always verify website URLs before logging in
- Check for HTTPS and lock symbol in browser
- Use strong and unique passwords
- Install antivirus or security software
- Never share OTP, passwords, or personal details
- Avoid downloading files from untrusted sources
- Regularly monitor your bank transactions
- Log out from accounts on shared or public devices
- Be cautious while using public Wi-Fi networks
- Do not trust messages that create urgency or fear
- Keep your mobile apps and system updated regularly
- Verify sender email address before replying


UPI / Financial Safety Tips:
- Do not scan unknown QR codes
- Never share your UPI PIN with anyone
- Double-check receiver details before sending money
- Always verify payment requests before approving
- Use official banking apps only

Report Cybercrime:
- National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
